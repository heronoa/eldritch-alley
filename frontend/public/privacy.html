Privacy Policy — Eldritch Alley: Tactics

Last updated: October 17, 2026

This policy explains which personal data Eldritch Alley: Tactics ("the game") collects, why it collects it, how long it keeps it and which rights you have, under the Brazilian General Data Protection Law (Law No. 13,709/2018, "LGPD").

1. Who is responsible for your data

The game is an independent project maintained by Heron Oliveira Amaral, CNPJ 58.978.216/0001-06, headquartered in Belém, Pará, Brazil, acting as the controller of the data.

Contact for any privacy matter: suporte@heronoa.com.br.

2. Which data we collect

Data Where it comes from What it is for
Display name, email, profile picture and account identifier From the sign-in provider you choose (GitHub, Google or the deployment's single sign-on), with your authorization; or from you, when you sign up with an email address Creating and identifying your account
Password, when you create the account with an email address You Signing in; stored only as a one-way hash, which does not allow it to be read
Agents and inventory Generated by the game Saving your squad (for example, the starting Sniper, Wizard and Priest)
Match history Generated by the game Showing your history and results, and allowing matches to be replayed
Messages sent through the contact form You Answering your message
Preference for receiving new-version emails You, when you tick the option Sending new-version notices, only if you accept them
IP address, access date and time, and technical browser data Generated automatically Security, abuse prevention and error correction

We do not collect payment data, documents, precise location or sensitive data. The IP address of contact messages is stored only as a hash, which serves to limit abuse and does not identify you directly.

3. Legal bases (art. 7 of the LGPD)

  • Performance of a contract: account, agents and match history, which are necessary for you to play.
  • Consent: new-version emails. You may withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legitimate interest: access logs, security and abuse prevention, and answering the contact form.
  • Compliance with a legal obligation: keeping access logs as required by the Brazilian Civil Rights Framework for the Internet (Law No. 12,965/2014, art. 15).

4. Cookies

We use only essential cookies, necessary to keep you signed in and to protect the session. We use no advertising cookies and no third-party tracking.

5. With whom we share data

We do not sell or rent your data. It is shared only with providers the game needs in order to work:

  • GitHub, Google and the deployment's single sign-on, for signing in, under each one's privacy policy.
  • Infrastructure providers where the game is hosted: our own server in Brazil and Amazon Web Services, region us-east-1 (Northern Virginia).
  • Cloudflare, for networking, security and spam protection.
  • Zoho, for sending email.
  • Authorities, when required by law or by a court order.

Some of these providers may process data outside Brazil, including in the United States. In those cases the transfer follows art. 33 of the LGPD, with providers that adopt compatible protection guarantees.

6. How long we keep data

Data Period
Account, agents and match history For as long as the account exists. When you delete the account, personal data is erased within 30 days; matches may be kept in anonymized form for the game's statistics.
Contact messages Up to 12 months after the reply
New-version email preference Until you withdraw consent
Access logs (IP, date and time) 6 months, under the Brazilian Civil Rights Framework for the Internet

7. Your rights (art. 18 of the LGPD)

You may, at any time:

  • confirm whether we process your data and access it;
  • correct incomplete or outdated data;
  • request anonymization, blocking or deletion of unnecessary data;
  • request portability of your data;
  • request deletion of your account and of data processed on the basis of consent;
  • know with whom we share your data;
  • withdraw your consent.

To exercise these rights, write to suporte@heronoa.com.br. We reply within 15 days. You may also complain to the Brazilian National Data Protection Authority (ANPD).

To stop receiving new-version emails, use the link in every email or untick the option in your profile.

8. Security

We adopt technical measures to protect your data, such as encrypted connections (HTTPS), passwords stored only as a one-way hash, restricted access to the servers and the database, and backups. No system is entirely immune to failure. If a security incident may put you at risk, we will notify the affected people and the ANPD, in accordance with the LGPD.

9. Children and teenagers

The game is not aimed at children under 12, and we do not intentionally collect their data. If you are responsible for a child and believe they created an account, write to suporte@heronoa.com.br so the account can be deleted.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the version in force. Relevant changes will be announced in the game or by email.
